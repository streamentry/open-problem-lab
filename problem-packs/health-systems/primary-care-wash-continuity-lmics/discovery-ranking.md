# Discovery Ranking

## Selection Method

I screened uncovered problem areas against four tests: quantified scale, a plausible intervention or measurement path, evidence that current effort is insufficient relative to the decision, and a named decision-maker who could act on a verified result. I checked the live portfolio of 120 packs across 16 domains on 5 September 2026. The ranking is a strategic screen; the candidates are not directly comparable and the selected problem is framed as a measurement wedge rather than a claim that one intervention is best.

## Selection Decision

The selected candidate is **primary-care water and hand-hygiene continuity in low- and middle-income health facilities**. It clears the scale test without pretending that all people served by a facility are harmed by one specific failure. It clears the tractability test because the measurement standards, facility-audit instruments, WASH FIT improvement cycle, and water-quality methods already exist. It clears the neglectedness test because current reporting still lacks a current global basic-service estimate for the 2023 reference year, and country action has not translated into sufficient financing or comparable continuity data. Most importantly, it names a decision: whether a Ministry of Health or funder should finance source infrastructure, storage and pumping, consumables, maintenance, or a better measurement system at a named primary-care facility network.

The first task is deliberately narrow. It inventories sources and separates static service levels, continuity, quality, quantity, point-of-care function, operational improvement, clinical outcomes, and missingness. It does not estimate a global causal effect of WASH, rank facilities, or issue a procurement recommendation.
