# Dataset Inventory

## Measure Families

Palliative-care access is a cascade, not one number. These families answer different questions and must not be collapsed:

| Measure family                | Question answered                                                                                                  | Common failure                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Population need               | Who meets the stated serious-health-related-suffering or palliative-care need definition?                          | A death, diagnosis, or last-year-of-life proxy is treated as the complete need denominator. |
| Service supply                | Which public, private, community, and home services exist, where, and under what scope?                            | A directory entry or policy is treated as an operating service.                             |
| Facility readiness            | Can a facility provide the defined service today with trained staff, guidance, medicines, equipment, and referral? | A facility reports a service but has no observed readiness or referral fallback.            |
| Referral and first contact    | Was a person referred, reached, and assessed by an appropriate service?                                            | A referral form or appointment is counted as care received.                                 |
| Continuing care               | Did symptom support, communication, caregiver support, and follow-up continue across time and settings?            | One encounter or discharge is treated as continuity.                                        |
| Medicine access               | Were the relevant medicines authorized, procured, available, affordable, dispensed, and used within scope?         | Morphine consumption or a single stock check is treated as complete access.                 |
| Person and caregiver outcomes | Did care match the person's goals and affect reported symptoms, function, dignity, or caregiver burden?            | Provider activity is substituted for patient-defined outcome or preference.                 |
| Equity and safety             | Who is missing because of geography, age, diagnosis, disability, poverty, migration, language, stigma, or consent? | A national average hides excluded groups and unsafe disclosure.                             |

## Required Field Crosswalk

Any country or district extract should preserve these fields, even when they are missing:

1. `need_definition`: age, diagnosis or suffering criteria, time window, and whether the estimate is all-need or last-year-of-life.
2. `population_frame`: geography, population source, sampling design, weights, inclusion/exclusion, and reference date.
3. `service_identity`: provider, ownership, facility or community location, level of care, operating status, scope, and named referral destination.
4. `readiness`: trained staff, guidance, privacy, essential medicines, stock date, equipment, opening hours, and observed or reported status.
5. `episode`: referral timestamp, first contact, assessment, care plan, follow-up, transfer, closure, loss to follow-up, and responsible actor.
6. `medicine`: product and formulation, legal authorization, procurement, facility availability, stockout duration, price/affordability, dispensing, and indication if known.
7. `person_and_caregiver`: consent, patient-defined goals, symptom/function measure, caregiver role and burden, complaints, safeguarding, and authorized disclosure.
8. `missingness_and_equity`: missing reason, non-response, attrition, language, disability, age, diagnosis, sex/gender where appropriate, rurality, poverty, migration, conflict exposure, and privacy boundary.

Unknown values must remain unknown. A policy, directory, facility contact, opioid statistic, or death record must not be imputed into another measure family.

## Inventory Decision Rule

A source is **usable** only for the specific measure family it can observe with a recoverable definition, grain, date, denominator, and missingness rule. It is **limited** when it is valuable context but cannot support a complete cascade field. It is **rejected** for a proposed comparison when the source is stale, inaccessible, non-representative for the intended claim, or its measure is routinely mistaken for a different family.

The next source-inventory task should produce one country-bounded crosswalk using at least one population source, one facility or service source, one medicine source, one routine or episode source, and one patient/caregiver source. It should report the missing joins instead of filling them with assumptions.
