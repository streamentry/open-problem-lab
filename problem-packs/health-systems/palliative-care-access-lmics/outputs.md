# Outputs

## Accepted Output Types

- dated source and dataset inventories
- need-denominator and measure-family crosswalks
- facility, service, referral, medicine, and continuity data dictionaries
- country-bounded measurement protocols with named decision-makers and non-use cases
- reproducible negative results showing that a proposed comparison is not valid
- replicated analyses that report missingness, equity, consent, and uncertainty

## Not An Output Of This Pack

This pack does not produce individual treatment advice, prescriptions, prognoses, opioid dosing, automated clinical triage, provider rankings, country blame lists, or claims of mortality or hospitalization benefit. A measurement result is not a clinical order.

## Decision-Use Contract

An allocation-facing output must state:

1. the named Ministry of Health, payer, regulator, or funder decision-maker
2. the exact benefits-package, workforce, service-location, procurement, or research-allocation decision
3. the evidence threshold and time at which the decision occurs
4. the population, facility, service, medicine, patient, and caregiver measures used
5. what is observed, inferred, assumed, and unknown
6. who could benefit, who bears cost or risk, and who is missing
7. the independent replication, domain review, medicines review, red-team review, and field-reality review status
8. the stop or non-use rule when evidence is incomplete, unsafe, non-comparable, or not accepted by the relevant authority

The preferred result may be “do not allocate on this evidence yet.”
