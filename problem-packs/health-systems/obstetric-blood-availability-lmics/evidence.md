## Evidence Notes

### Postpartum haemorrhage burden and roadmap

Use WHO's 2023 roadmap announcement for global burden framing and for the fact that blood is one component of facility readiness. It does not locate the supply-chain failure or prove that blood access alone determines mortality.

### National blood-system indicators

Use the 2026 WHO blood-safety summary and global status report for national supply, governance, screening, and processing context. Do not treat donation rates as facility-level stock or emergency access.

### Facility measurement instruments

SARA and HHFA are the strongest starting points for standardized facility measurement. Their readiness and interruption indicators are measurement inputs, not validated predictors of maternal survival or clinical response time.

### Regional shortfall

The WHO AFRO 2021 release supports the existence of a severe regional supply gap and its relevance to obstetric bleeding. Its pandemic-period context and regional grain require current country or facility validation.

## Evidence Quality Rule

Every quantitative output must preserve source grain, observation window, numerator, denominator, and missingness. A national blood statistic may frame the problem, but it cannot be promoted into a facility ranking or a clinical recommendation without facility-level evidence, domain review, red-team review, and replication.
