# Outputs

## Accepted Outputs

No accepted outputs exist yet. This is deliberate. The pack is scoped, not solved.

## Candidate Output Types

- Source and indicator inventory.
- Facility-level measurement crosswalk.
- Stock-interruption and readiness data-quality audit.
- Reproducible descriptive analysis of supply and referral timing.
- Red-team review of facility comparisons or allocation interpretations.
- Field-reality review of whether an evidence artifact arrives in time to change a named health-system decision.

## Publication Rule

Published outputs must state what they are not. A facility-readiness measure is not a clinical transfusion protocol, a maternal-survival prediction, a public ranking, or an instruction to divert blood from another facility.
