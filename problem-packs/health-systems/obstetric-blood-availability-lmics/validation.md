# Validation

## Validation Layers

| Layer           | Gate                                                                                            |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Structure       | `pnpm validate` passes schemas and problem-pack completeness checks                             |
| Evidence        | Every quantitative statement links to a dated evidence record with source grain and limitations |
| Reproducibility | Any quantitative artifact includes rerunnable steps and preserves indicator definitions         |
| Domain review   | Transfusion-medicine and maternal-health reviewers assess measurement and interpretation        |
| Safety review   | Red-team review blocks unsafe allocation, ranking, or clinical inference                        |
| Replication     | Quantitative or operational outputs are independently reproduced before acceptance              |

## Minimum Measurement Requirements

Every facility-level comparison must document:

- Facility identifier, ownership, and level of care.
- Blood product and availability definition.
- Observation date or window.
- Interruption, request, cross-match, issue, transfusion, and referral timestamps where available.
- Obstetric case definition and denominator.
- Missingness, reporting lag, and private-facility coverage.
- Whether the measure is observed readiness, reported availability, or actual emergency performance.

## Non-Use Gate

No national donation statistic may be used as a proxy for facility time-to-transfusion. No facility score may be published as a clinical or resource-allocation recommendation until domain review, red-team review, and replication are complete.
