# Validation

## Validation Layers

| Layer           | Gate                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Structure       | `pnpm validate` passes schemas and problem-pack completeness checks.                                                                                  |
| Evidence        | Every quantified statement has a dated source, source identity, method, and explicit limitation.                                                      |
| Definitions     | Connection, current function, reliability, backup, capacity, service-area reach, device function, service use, and patient outcome are not conflated. |
| Reproducibility | A reviewer can rerun classifications and calculations from documented fields, rules, environment, and input hash.                                     |
| Review          | Health-systems domain, field-reality, and red-team reviewers approve high-risk interpretations.                                                       |
| Replication     | Quantitative or allocation-facing outputs are independently reproduced before publication.                                                            |

## Baseline Requirements

Any facility-level analysis must report:

- Facility identifier or an irreversible pseudonym, type, ownership, location grain, and sampling frame.
- Service area or department observed, not only a compound-wide label.
- Measurement mode and observation window, including outage threshold and regular service hours.
- Primary source, backup source, fuel or battery status, capacity, maintenance responsibility, and repair delay where available.
- Numerator, denominator, missingness, exclusions, weights, and administrative-boundary version.
- The named service or device whose function is being assessed.
- Non-use cases, especially the distinction between electricity reliability and clinical outcome.

## Failure Rule

If a field cannot distinguish ownership from function, a survey cannot expose its denominator and observation window, or a source cannot state what service or device was observed, it cannot enter a canonical facility-reliability estimate. It may remain as limited evidence or a method-design reference.
