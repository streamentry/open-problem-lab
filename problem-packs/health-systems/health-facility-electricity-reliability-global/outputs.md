# Outputs

## Accepted Outputs

No accepted outputs exist yet.

## Candidate Output Types

- Dated source inventory with facility- and service-area reliability-definition crosswalk.
- Missingness, denominator, and measurement-mode audit for a national or network facility assessment.
- Replicated facility-level baseline separating connection, function, outages, backup, load, service area, and device function.
- Component-failure table that preserves facility level, ownership, geography, season, and observation window.
- Red-team memo for allocation, procurement, clinical, privacy, and facility-blame interpretations.
- Field-reality review naming one responsible infrastructure decision and one explicit non-use.

## Publication Rule

No output may rank facilities or countries, prescribe a grid, solar, battery, generator, or maintenance intervention, claim clinical or mortality benefit, or disclose infrastructure vulnerabilities until health-systems domain review, field-reality review, red-team review, and independent replication are complete.
