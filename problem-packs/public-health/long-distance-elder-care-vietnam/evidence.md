## Evidence Notes

### UNFPA Viet Nam 2026

Use for current national framing on family-care dependence, gendered care responsibility, limited integrated long-term care, and policy direction. The page does not expose the underlying estimation tables, so its percentages must not become household targeting thresholds.

### UNFPA Viet Nam 2022

Use for documented pilot scope, care-planning products, training counts, and the explicit statement that more evidence is needed. Do not treat the pilot report as an impact evaluation.

### WHO ICOPE

Use as the care-domain and person-centred workflow framework. It supports coordinated assessment and caregiver support; it does not validate remote monitoring or any Vietnamese service operator.

### World Bank And JICA 2021

Use for dated demographic and long-term-care policy framing. Do not use its projections as current counts or its policy recommendation as proof of a delivery model.

### World Bank ISHC Field Report 2024

Use to establish that a community-based home-care mechanism has operated in six provinces and to name candidate service functions. Treat program totals and participant stories as limited field evidence without a counterfactual.

### Decision 1648/QD-TTg

Use to establish national policy authorization for expanding intergenerational self-help clubs through 2035. Verify local implementation separately.

### 2019 Population and Housing Census

Use for the population frame, household context, migration, and disability proxies. Public tabulations do not identify a timely family responder or a functioning local service.

### Population Change and Family Planning Survey 2021 older-person module

The direct report is usable for older-person care-needs and care-receipt context: it documents a nationally representative 2021 sample across all 63 provinces and cities and reports ADL need, care receipt, unmet care need, preferred care setting, and willingness to pay. The report also records the decisive limits for this pack: some living-arrangement categories are merged, ADLs are self-assessed, care quality is not measured, and no family travel time or local response-closure evidence is provided. Do not use it alone to construct a timely-response denominator.

### Vietnam Time-Use Survey 2022

Use for unpaid elder-care time and caregiver-burden context. The respondent universe is ages 15–64, so it cannot stand in for older-person need or consent.

### National Survey on People with Disabilities 2016

Use for functional-limitation measurement only, with explicit staleness and construct-boundary checks. Disability is not synonymous with care dependence.

### National Internal Migration Survey 2015

Use to locate migration and service-access context that motivates direct family-distance measurement. Migration status is not a response-time variable.

## Strongest Contrary Evidence

No reviewed source proves that a remote family dashboard, wearable, or scheduled call improves health outcomes or emergency survival in Viet Nam. No reviewed source provides a current denominator combining:

- older-person living arrangement
- family travel time
- functional need
- consent preference
- local responder availability

That absence blocks market-size, safety, and impact claims.

## Suggested Next Issue

Review the completed source inventory, then scope the care-gap denominator task around the 2021 older-person module while leaving family travel time, consent preference, and local responder availability explicitly unknown when unobserved. Do not open the issue automatically.

## Evidence Quality Rule

Evidence is accepted only when the source, method, limitations, date, and geographic grain are explicit enough for a reviewer to attack.
