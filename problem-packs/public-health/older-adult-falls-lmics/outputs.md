# Outputs

## Accepted Output Types

- dated source and dataset inventories
- fall-event, injury-severity, time-to-care, rehabilitation, and functional-outcome crosswalks
- facility and community surveillance data dictionaries
- prevention-intervention fidelity and transportability reviews
- country- or district-bounded measurement protocols with named decision-makers and non-use cases
- reproducible negative results showing that a proposed comparison is not valid
- replicated analyses that report uncertainty, missingness, equity, consent, and residual risk

## Not An Output Of This Pack

This pack does not produce individual fall-risk scores, diagnosis, medication changes, exercise prescriptions, clinical triage, home-safety orders, provider rankings, care-denial rules, or claims that one intervention reduces mortality or disability everywhere.

## Decision-Use Contract

An allocation-facing output must state:

1. the named Ministry of Health, district, rehabilitation, housing, transport, or funder decision-maker
2. the exact surveillance, prevention, referral, fracture-care, rehabilitation, housing, or research-allocation decision
3. the population, event, injury, service, functional, and equity measures used
4. what is observed, inferred, assumed, and unknown
5. the evidence threshold, timing, and cost of the decision
6. who could benefit, who bears cost or risk, and who is missing
7. the domain, intervention, red-team, field-reality, and replication status
8. the stop or non-use rule when evidence is incomplete, unsafe, non-comparable, or not accepted by the relevant authority

The preferred result may be “do not allocate on this evidence yet.”
