# Dataset Inventory

## Measure Families

Falls are an event-to-function pathway, not one indicator. These families answer different questions and must not be collapsed:

| Measure family        | Question answered                                                                                                                         | Common failure                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Population fall event | How many older adults fall, how often, and in what setting?                                                                               | Facility presentations or one-time self-report are treated as population incidence.                        |
| Fall-related injury   | What injury, severity, admission, disability, or death followed the fall?                                                                 | A fall without injury is combined with a serious injury or fracture.                                       |
| Time-to-care          | How long elapsed from event to care-seeking, departure, transport, arrival, assessment, treatment, transfer, or rehabilitation?           | One “arrival time” is treated as the whole delay or as a causal mechanism.                                 |
| Prevention exposure   | Which intervention, home modification, education, exercise, medication review, or environmental change was actually offered and received? | A programme invitation or attendance is treated as exposure to every component.                            |
| Functional recovery   | Did mobility, activities, participation, symptoms, quality of life, or patient-defined goals change after the fall?                       | Discharge status or fall recurrence is treated as long-term function.                                      |
| Service capacity      | Which facilities, transport services, rehabilitation providers, and community workers exist and are ready?                                | A directory, ambulance number, or facility label is treated as a functioning pathway.                      |
| Equity and safety     | Who is missed or harmed by the measurement or intervention?                                                                               | Older age, disability, poverty, rurality, sex, or living arrangement is converted into blame or exclusion. |

## Required Field Crosswalk

Any country- or district-level extract should preserve these fields, even when missing:

1. `person_frame`: age definition, sex/gender where appropriate, residence, sampling frame, weights, consent, and privacy boundary.
2. `fall_event`: date/time, definition, recurrence, mechanism, height or level, place, activity, intent, witness/source, and recall window.
3. `injury`: body region, injury type, severity definition, clinical assessment, admission, surgery/fracture care, disability, and death.
4. `time_to_care`: event-to-decision, decision-to-departure, transport, arrival, assessment, treatment, transfer, rehabilitation referral, and outcome timestamps.
5. `prevention`: intervention identity, allocation, eligibility, delivery, attendance or exposure, fidelity, co-interventions, adverse events, and withdrawal.
6. `function`: baseline and follow-up mobility, activities, participation, quality of life, patient-defined goals, caregiver burden, and instrument/version.
7. `service`: facility or community provider, level, operating status, staffing, equipment, transport, referral owner, hours, catchment, and closure state.
8. `missingness_equity`: missing reason, non-response, attrition, failed linkage, rurality, poverty, disability, age, language, living arrangement, and safeguarding event.
