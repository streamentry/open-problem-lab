# Discovery Ranking

## Selection Method

I screened uncovered problem areas against four tests: quantified burden, a plausible measurement or intervention path, evidence of under-service or under-measurement, and a named decision that verified evidence could change. The ranking is a strategic screen, not a claim that the candidates are directly comparable. Existing packs were checked across 16 live domains and 119 packs on 3 September 2026.

## Candidate Notes

Older-adult falls ranked first because the burden is high, concentrated in LMICs, and connected to decisions that are both concrete and reversible: improve surveillance, test a prevention pathway, shorten a care delay, or extend rehabilitation. The WHO framework, six-country SAGE analysis, prospective African registry, and recent rural-China trial show that the problem is neither unmeasurable nor ready for a universal claim. The wedge is to make event, injury, delay, function, prevention fidelity, and equity auditable in one bounded setting.

Burns are an important alternative with a large LMIC burden and known prevention routes, but clean-cooking, household-air-pollution, and emergency-care packs already own much of the surrounding evidence. Wheelchair access has a clear 80-million-person need estimate and strong WHO tools, but the repository's disability, hearing, and blindness packs create a duplication risk. Neonatal bacterial infection has extreme severity and a new WHO diagnostic product profile, but it is already better served by the neonatal, maternal-child, and AMR pipelines. Pesticide poisoning remains a serious measurement problem, but the most memorable global estimate was retracted and the current verified denominator is unknown; it fails the discovery bar until the measurement layer is repaired.

## Selection Decision

Older-adult fall injury prevention and post-fall function is the strongest uncovered candidate. The first task is intentionally a source inventory that can end in a negative result. No individual risk score, clinical recommendation, or universal programme claim is proposed.
