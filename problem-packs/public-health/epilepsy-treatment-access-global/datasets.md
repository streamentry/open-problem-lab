# Dataset and source inventory

This is an initial inventory for scoping. A source is not “usable” merely because it contains an epilepsy keyword. The source must expose a grain, time reference, denominator, case definition, and access path that a reviewer can inspect.

| Source family                                                         | Typical grain and time                               | Initial classification                                                    | What it can support                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | What it cannot prove                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WHO epilepsy fact sheet and global report                             | Global, periodically updated synthesis               | Usable for framing; limited for local denominators                        | Burden framing, treatment-gap definitions, primary-care feasibility, and named measurement boundaries ([who-epilepsy-fact-sheet-2024](evidence.md#who-epilepsy-fact-sheet-2024), [who-epilepsy-public-health-imperative-2019](evidence.md#who-epilepsy-public-health-imperative-2019)) | A country or district's observed prevalence, medicine continuity, or treatment outcome                                |
| WHO neurological-medicine access report                               | Global and system-level framework, published 2024    | Usable for measurement design; limited for observed care                  | Medicine-access dimensions, system barriers, tracer-condition logic, and decision-maker mapping ([who-neurological-medicines-access-2024](evidence.md#who-neurological-medicines-access-2024))                                                                                         | A local stockout rate, patient affordability result, or causal effect of an access intervention                       |
| Household or population-based epilepsy surveys                        | Household or community cluster, survey wave          | Potentially usable when case definition and sampling frame are documented | Active-epilepsy or seizure-history denominator, care-seeking, treatment status, stigma, and unmet need                                                                                                                                                                                 | Complete national coverage when survey geography is narrow; clinical diagnosis when screening is self-reported        |
| Primary-care and district health registers                            | Facility or catchment, monthly/quarterly/yearly      | Potentially usable after linkage and missingness audit                    | Diagnosed cases, visits, dispensing, follow-up, referral, and reported outcomes within the system                                                                                                                                                                                      | People who never enter the system, care outside the system, or population prevalence without a defensible denominator |
| Pharmacy and facility medicine surveys using WHO/HAI methods          | Outlet or facility, survey day or short field window | Limited but useful for access components                                  | Product presence, price, affordability proxy, and facility-tier comparison ([cameron-antiepileptic-medicines-46-countries-2012](evidence.md#cameron-antiepileptic-medicines-46-countries-2012), [hailemariam-asm-addis-ababa-2023](evidence.md#hailemariam-asm-addis-ababa-2023))      | Persistent stock continuity, dispensing to patients, adherence, or seizure control                                    |
| Procurement, logistics, and stock records                             | Product/facility/month or transaction                | Potentially usable if product identifiers and stock rules are explicit    | Orders, receipts, stock-on-hand, stockouts, lead times, and facility-tier continuity                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Patient possession, correct prescribing, clinical effectiveness, or reasons for non-use                               |
| Epilepsy clinic registries and referral records                       | Patient or episode, facility and referral date       | Limited unless population catchment and deduplication are documented      | Diagnosis, specialist review, treatment change, referral completion, and follow-up within the registry                                                                                                                                                                                 | Community prevalence, people treated elsewhere, or equitable coverage across the catchment                            |
| Randomized or quasi-experimental implementation studies               | Trial cluster or programme site, follow-up period    | Usable for tested delivery mechanisms; limited for generalization         | Whether a defined care model changes observed adherence or seizure outcomes in the study setting ([singh-home-based-epilepsy-care-2022](evidence.md#singh-home-based-epilepsy-care-2022))                                                                                              | A global effect size, cost, or scale-up recommendation                                                                |

| Search-result compilations, advocacy claims, and undated social posts | Unknown                                              | Rejected                                                                  | Nothing enters canonical evidence without source identity, date, and method                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Any burden, access, safety, or allocation claim                                                                       |

## Required fields before a source becomes usable

Every accepted source must document:

- case or exposure definition, including whether it measures active epilepsy, lifetime seizures, diagnosis, or treatment;
- numerator and denominator, including whether the denominator is population, screened people, clinic attendees, or medicine users;
- event date, reference period, and whether the measure is point-in-time, period, or cumulative;
- geographic and facility grain, catchment boundaries, and transfer handling;
- source access, license, missingness, and linkage limitations;
- whether the source observes policy, procurement, stock, dispensing, possession, adherence, seizure control, referral, or outcome.

No dataset in this inventory should be used for country ranking until those fields are complete and a replicator can reproduce the denominator.
