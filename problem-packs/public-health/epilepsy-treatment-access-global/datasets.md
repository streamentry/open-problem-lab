## Required fields before a source becomes usable

Every accepted source must document:

- case or exposure definition, including whether it measures active epilepsy, lifetime seizures, diagnosis, or treatment;
- numerator and denominator, including whether the denominator is population, screened people, clinic attendees, or medicine users;
- event date, reference period, and whether the measure is point-in-time, period, or cumulative;
- geographic and facility grain, catchment boundaries, and transfer handling;
- source access, license, missingness, and linkage limitations;
- whether the source observes policy, procurement, stock, dispensing, possession, adherence, seizure control, referral, or outcome.

No dataset in this inventory should be used for country ranking until those fields are complete and a replicator can reproduce the denominator. In particular, a population survey, modeled estimate, outlet stock observation, clinic register, and patient-reported seizure outcome are different evidence objects even when they share the word “treatment.”

## Source-inventory update, August 2026

The inventory now has dated evidence records for five previously unrecorded source families:

- `gbd-epilepsy-burden-2021` is usable for modeled burden context but limited for an observed active-epilepsy denominator or any care stage.
- `bangladesh-national-epilepsy-survey-2017` is usable as a population-based survey example with an explicit sample, response, active-epilepsy classification, and treatment-gap denominator, but its summary does not expose the full instrument or longitudinal linkage.
- `wagner-epilepsy-care-cascade-rural-south-africa-2020` is usable as a population-based cascade and adherence design example, with explicit limits on subtype, catchment, and missing stages.
- `primary-care-epilepsy-follow-up-rural-ethiopia-2009` is limited but useful for register-based default, transfer, migration, death, and return-to-care handling; it must not be treated as current performance evidence.
- `who-igap-epilepsy-2023` is usable for mapping a local measurement artifact to an accountable global policy and indicator framework, but it is not observed service coverage.

The source-inventory done condition is met for this task: the table classifies more than seven source families and names the active-epilepsy denominator, diagnosis, stock or dispensing, possession or adherence, seizure outcome, referral, geographic grain, time reference, and missingness boundary for each family.
