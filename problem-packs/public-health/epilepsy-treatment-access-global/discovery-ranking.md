## Selection judgment

The strongest objection is that epilepsy already has WHO reports, a global action plan, and implementation pilots. That objection survives. The selection does not claim that epilepsy is institutionally invisible. It claims that a verified, reusable measurement layer is still missing: decision-makers need to know whether a local treatment gap is driven by the denominator, case finding, diagnosis, medicine continuity, follow-up, stigma, or referral before choosing an intervention. That is a narrower and more falsifiable problem than “epilepsy is neglected.”

The pack therefore starts with source classification and denominator discipline. It does not begin with a model, an AI diagnostic tool, a country ranking, or a treatment recommendation.
