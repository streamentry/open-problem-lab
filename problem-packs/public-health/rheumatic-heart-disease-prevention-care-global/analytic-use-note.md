# Analytic Use Note

This pack is for evidence review and health-system measurement. It is not a clinical protocol.

Any programme-facing artifact must name:

- the ministry, programme, funder, or service manager who would use it;
- the concrete allocation or measurement decision;
- the data time window and stage-specific lead time, if relevant;
- the cost of falsely identifying a bottleneck and the cost of missing one;
- the people and facilities absent from the data;
- the public-health, red-team, field-reality, and replication reviews required before use.

A cascade gap is not a diagnosis. A registry is not a population denominator. A stockout is not proof that a patient missed a dose. A screening result is not a confirmed case. A programme-facing output that erases any of these distinctions is blocked.
