# Outputs

## Accepted Outputs

No accepted outputs exist yet. This is a scoped discovery pack, not a clinical protocol or an allocation recommendation.

## Candidate Output Types

- Source inventory and measure dictionary.
- De-identified or synthetic cascade data specification.
- One-country or one-network baseline with stage-specific denominators and missingness.
- Linkage audit showing how private care, transfers, or migration change the apparent bottleneck.
- Replicated comparison of a hospital-based and primary-care delivery pathway.
- Red-team and field-reality review of any proposed programme-facing artifact.

## Publication Rule

Any published output must name its decision user, decision, time horizon, data grain, and non-use cases. A cascade table is not a diagnosis, a treatment protocol, a country league table, or an automatic funding order. No field-facing output is publishable until domain, red-team, field-reality, and replication gates are satisfied where applicable.
