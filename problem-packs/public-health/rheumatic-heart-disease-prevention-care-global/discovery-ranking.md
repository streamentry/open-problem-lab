## Ranking Judgment

RHD is not selected because it is the largest problem in this table. It is selected because it combines a large, young, LMIC-concentrated burden with named prevention and treatment components, a documented history of underfunded research, and a narrow measurement question that a ministry or funder could actually use. The strongest counterargument is that a WHO guideline and existing registries may already make a new pack redundant. The response is that neither a guideline nor a referred registry answers where a named health system loses people between primary prevention, prophylaxis, diagnosis, referral, and valve care; the first task is explicitly allowed to find that no usable cascade data exist.
