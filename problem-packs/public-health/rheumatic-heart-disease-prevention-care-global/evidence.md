# Evidence Ledger

## Current Evidence Records

The machine-readable ledger is `evidence.json`. The records below are the initial discovery evidence, not accepted claims about country performance.

### WHO RHD Fact Sheet — `who-rhd-fact-sheet-2025`

Use for current global burden framing and for the fact that WHO's response description includes prevention, screening, antibiotics, monitoring, and care. Do not use it as a country-level cascade denominator or as proof that a recommended service is delivered.

Suggested next task: build a source inventory that tests whether any named country can observe the stages separately.

### WHO Guideline — `who-rhd-guideline-2024`

Use to define the intervention and measurement vocabulary: primary prevention, secondary prophylaxis, adherence support, screening, and rheumatic-fever management. Do not treat guideline recommendations as coverage or as patient-level advice.

Suggested next task: create a measure dictionary that distinguishes policy, stock, dispensing, administration, attendance, and adherence.

### WHA71.14 — `wha71-14-rheumatic-heart-disease-2018`

Use as evidence that burden measurement, surveillance, agreed measures, follow-up, workforce, and reliable tools are explicit policy needs. It is not an implementation report.

Suggested next task: map each resolution request to an observable data field and a non-observable proxy.

### Macleod et al. — `macleod-rhd-underfunding-2019`

Use for historical comparative R&D neglect only. The funding-to-burden index is not a live funding monitor, an impact estimate, or a reason to assume that every new dollar has high value.

Suggested next task: refresh funding and implementation-effort evidence before making a current neglectedness claim.

### REMEDY registry — `remedy-rhd-registry-2016`

Use to show the severity of outcomes among people who reach participating clinical centres. Do not generalise the referred cohort to the population or use it to infer upstream prevention coverage.

Suggested next task: identify a data source that includes people lost before specialist referral.

### Northern Uganda decentralisation — `minja-uganda-rhd-decentralisation-2025`

Use as a tractability signal and as a source of candidate implementation variables: facility level, training, supply, transport, pain, registry, adherence, and acceptability. Do not treat it as a global intervention recommendation.

Suggested next task: replicate the cascade and cost boundaries in a second setting before comparing models.

## Evidence Quality Rule

Evidence enters canonical framing only when the source, date, method, limitation, and confidence are explicit. The initial pack contains no accepted claim and no clinical instruction.
