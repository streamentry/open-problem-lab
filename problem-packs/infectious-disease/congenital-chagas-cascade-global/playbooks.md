# Playbooks

## Analyst Playbook

1. Read `problem.json`, `evidence.json`, and `tasks.json`.
2. Define the cascade stage before selecting a dataset.
3. Keep policy, procurement, screening, diagnosis, treatment, completion, and follow-up as separate events.
4. De-identify records before linkage and document unmatched records.
5. Treat all quantitative outputs as unverified until independently replicated.

## Reviewer Playbook

Ask:

1. What exact stage and denominator are measured?
2. Does the source observe the event or merely describe policy or availability?
3. Are test timing, repeat tests, and migration handled?
4. Could the output stigmatize women, migrants, or facilities?
5. What observation would falsify the bottleneck claim?

## Field-Reality Playbook

The named user must be a program decision-maker with authority over a budget, workflow, or reporting standard. Do not publish a map or ranking unless the user, decision, timing, appeal path, and non-use cases are explicit.
