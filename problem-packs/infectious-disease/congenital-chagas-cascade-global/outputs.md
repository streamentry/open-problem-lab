# Outputs

## Allowed V0 Outputs

- A source and measure inventory.
- A de-identified cascade table for a named country or migrant-serving network.
- A missingness and linkage audit.
- A replicated analytic bottleneck assessment for funding or program-design review.

## Blocked Outputs

- Individual clinical advice or diagnosis.
- Public rankings of facilities or countries without verified denominators.
- Screening or treatment instructions.
- Claims that a positive maternal test predicts an infant outcome without appropriate diagnostic evidence.
