# Evidence Ledger

## Current Evidence Records

The machine-readable ledger is `evidence.json`.

## Evidence Notes

The WHO burden and campaign pages justify the problem's scale and pathway framing. They do not supply observed country-level completion rates. The 2024 WHO NTD report is useful for locating program-status evidence, but “initiated” is not “covered,” and coverage is not “completed treatment.” The 2011 technical recommendations are retained as a pathway reference only; a contributor must check whether current national guidance, diagnostics, and medicine policy supersede them.

## Evidence Quality Rule

Do not combine the global infection estimate, the estimated maternal-fetal transmission rate, and a national birth count into a local case estimate without a documented model and independent replication. A cascade claim must identify the stage, denominator, time window, and missingness mechanism.
