# Validation

## Validation Layers

| Layer           | Gate                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Structure       | `pnpm validate` passes schemas, source-identity checks, and canonical-file completeness.                                                           |
| Evidence        | Every burden, pathway, coverage, or implementation assertion has a dated evidence record with method and limitations.                              |
| Reproducibility | Quantitative cascade outputs include input hash, environment, rerunnable steps, and explicit denominator rules.                                    |
| Privacy         | Records are de-identified before linkage; no personal, maternal, newborn, household, migration, or clinical identifiers enter canonical artifacts. |
| Review          | Infectious-disease domain, red-team, and field-reality reviewers approve or block according to safety risk.                                        |
| Replication     | Quantitative bottleneck claims are independently reproduced before allocation-facing use.                                                          |

## Baseline Requirements

Any cascade table must document:

- maternal screening eligibility denominator, test type, specimen date, result,
  repeat-test handling, and whether the source observes the event;
- prophylaxis eligibility basis, offer or initiation event, date, and unknown
  status without inferring adherence from procurement or dispensing;
- live-birth denominator, birth setting, birth date and time, and handling of
  home births, stillbirths, late registration, private care, and migrants;
- birth-dose administration date and time, timely-versus-late classification,
  unknown timing, product, and dose-record source;
- infant-series schedule reference, dose dates, completion definition, and the
  distinction between scheduled, received, documented, and unknown doses;
- impact measure definition, assay, specimen timing, age cohort, and the rule
  preventing impact inference from service inputs alone;
- duplicate episodes, unmatched records, reporting lag, administrative-boundary
  version, and missingness mechanism.

## Non-Use Gate

No output may be used to direct clinical care, coerce screening, deny services,
sanction a facility, or label a mother, migrant population, or country. The
output is for reviewed programme-design and funding decisions only until
field-reality review and independent replication are complete.

## Kill Conditions For V0

The discovery wedge should be narrowed or rejected if a named high-burden
country can already produce a representative, linked, independently auditable
table for all cascade stages, or if the proposed data sources cannot distinguish
timely administration from late administration and missing timing after a
reasonable audit. A large burden estimate alone cannot rescue a failed
measurement wedge.
