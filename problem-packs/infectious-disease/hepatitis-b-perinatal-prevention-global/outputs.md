# Outputs

## Allowed V0 Outputs

- A dated source inventory and cascade measure dictionary.
- A de-identified or synthetic country-level event table with explicit linkage
  and missingness fields.
- A replicated comparison of timely versus late birth-dose recording.
- A country or health-system bottleneck analysis suitable for reviewed funding
  or programme-design discussion.
- A red-team and field-reality review of proposed data products.

## Blocked Outputs

- Individual vaccination, diagnosis, or antiviral-treatment advice.
- A facility, country, mother, or migrant-population league table.
- A claim that vaccine procurement, cold-chain availability, stock, or policy
  adoption equals administration or protection.
- A claim that a positive maternal HBsAg result alone proves an infant outcome.
- Re-identifiable maternal, newborn, household, migration, or clinical data.
- A clinical or public-health action command before domain, red-team,
  field-reality, and replication gates are satisfied.

## Publication Rule

Every quantitative output must state its decision user, time window, event
definitions, denominator, missingness mechanism, uncertainty, non-use cases,
and replication status. “Coverage” without a timing and event definition is
not a publishable result in this pack.
