# Outputs

## Accepted Outputs

No accepted outputs exist yet. This is deliberate: the pack is scoped discovery work, not a confirmed global burden estimate or an etiologic finding.

## Candidate Output Types

- Cross-setting CKDu/CINAC source and case-definition matrix.
- Reproducible audit of persistent CKD versus transient AKI handling in agricultural-worker studies.
- Measurement dictionary for kidney phenotype and objective versus self-reported occupational/environmental exposures.
- Feasibility review for sentinel surveillance in two named agricultural or manual-worker settings.
- Red-team review of causal overreach, unsupported exposure bans, worker blame, and employment exclusion.

## Publication Rule

Every published output must say whether it measures CKD or CKDu, the persistence rule, the sampling frame, the exposure mode, and the missingness. It must not present total CKD burden as a CKDu denominator, a suspected exposure as a cause, or a surveillance signal as a clinical diagnosis or intervention order.
