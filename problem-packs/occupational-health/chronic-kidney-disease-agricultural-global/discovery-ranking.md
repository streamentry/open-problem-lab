## Selection judgment

The strongest objection is that CKDu research already exists and that the disease label itself may encode an unproven causal story. That objection survives and shapes the pack: CKDu is treated as an operational surveillance label, not as a confirmed etiology. The selected contribution is justified only by the documented measurement failure — inconsistent terminology, uncertain cross-region burden, difficulty separating onset from progression, and weak objective exposure capture. The first artifact must therefore be a source and case-definition crosswalk. If the crosswalk cannot establish comparability across two settings, the correct result is a negative measurement finding, not a global ranking or an intervention recommendation.
