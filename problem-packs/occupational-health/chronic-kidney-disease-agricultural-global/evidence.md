# Evidence Ledger

## Current Evidence Records

The machine-readable ledger is `evidence.json`.

## Evidence Notes

### WHO Kidney Disease Fact Sheet

Use this source for broad CKD scale and the persistent-kidney-disease diagnostic boundary. Do not use the 674 million estimate as a CKDu denominator. WHO's two-eGFR/90-day rule is a minimum persistence check, not an etiologic classification.

### ISN i3C Measurement Gaps

Use the 2026 ISN consortium report as direct evidence that CKDu research remains hard to compare: burden differences are poorly described, terminology is inconsistent, onset and progression are difficult to separate, and self-reported exposures are insufficient. This supports the pack's measurement wedge, not a causal hypothesis.

### ISN Global Kidney Health Atlas

Use the Atlas for kidney-care capacity and diagnostic-system context. The total kidney-disease estimate is not CKDu-specific, and survey readiness is not the same as functioning access for an agricultural worker.

### Sri Lanka Multilevel Case Definition

Use this paper as a regional case-definition template. A future crosswalk must preserve which elements are universal CKD criteria, which are regional exclusion rules, and which are local research additions.

### MANOS Occupational Cohort

Use MANOS as a design precedent for repeated eGFR testing, worksite observation, biological sampling, and industry comparison. It is not proof that heat, agrochemicals, dehydration, or any other exposure causes CKDu.

### CKDu-AGRI Tamil Nadu

Use this study to demonstrate the denominator effect of repeat testing: a substantial number of first low-eGFR observations normalized at remeasurement. Do not compare its 2.66% CKDu estimate with another study until testing intervals, assays, exclusion rules, and sampling frames are harmonized.

### Rural Sri Lanka CKDu Study

Use the 8.5% suspected-CKDu result as a bounded local signal and a prompt to inspect biomarkers, water, agricultural work, and exposure measurement. It is not a national or global burden estimate.

## Cross-Setting Evidence Rule

No cross-country prevalence or risk comparison may be added until the source inventory records:

- persistent kidney-function confirmation and repeat interval;
- assay, eGFR equation, and urine marker;
- traditional-risk-factor exclusions and CKDu/CINAC label;
- numerator, denominator, age and sex distribution, worker status, and sampling frame;
- objective versus self-reported exposure mode and recall window;
- transient AKI handling, missingness, and loss to follow-up; and
- whether the data can be accessed or independently replicated.

## Evidence Quality Rule

Evidence is not accepted because it names CKDu or reports a large percentage. It is accepted when the source, method, denominator, persistence rule, classification boundary, exposure mode, limitations, and confidence are explicit enough for a reviewer to attack.
