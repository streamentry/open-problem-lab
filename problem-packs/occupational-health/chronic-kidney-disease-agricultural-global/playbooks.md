# Playbooks

## Analyst Playbook

1. Start with `problem.json`, `evidence.json`, `datasets.md`, and `tasks.json`.
2. Select one scoped task.
3. Add evidence before adding claims or rankings.
4. Record the CKD persistence rule, eGFR method, urine marker, phenotype, exclusion criteria, denominator, and repeat interval for every study.
5. Separate CKDu/CINAC labels from traditional CKD, transient AKI, and unclassified low eGFR.
6. Record objective, biological, environmental, and self-reported exposures as different measurement modes.
7. Mark every causal or operational inference as reviewed, replicated, or blocked.

## Reviewer Playbook

Ask five questions:

1. What exactly is the disease label and can it be falsified?
2. What source proves the numerator, denominator, persistence, and phenotype?
3. Which findings are observed, which are associations, and which are hypotheses?
4. Could transient AKI, dehydration, assay error, selection, or missingness explain the result?
5. Who could misuse the result to blame workers, ban an input, restrict employment, or allocate care without causal evidence?

## Field-Reality Playbook

Do not ask whether a CKDu map is impressive. Ask whether it changes a named decision for a named user at a useful time. For this pack, the user may be a Ministry of Health NCD director, labor ministry, occupational-health service, or research funder. The first responsible decision is usually whether to fund better surveillance or exposure measurement, not whether to impose a work rule or identify a culprit.
